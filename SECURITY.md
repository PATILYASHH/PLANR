# Security policy

## Supported versions
The latest release on [Releases](https://github.com/PATILYASHH/PLANR/releases), and from 15 December 2026 the Google Play and F-Droid builds.

## Reporting a vulnerability
Please **don't open a public issue**. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting:
**Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository. Include the PLANR version, Android version and the steps to reproduce.

You'll get a reply within 7 days. Fixes for confirmed issues ship in the next release and are credited, with your permission.

## Scope
Especially interesting: ways to bypass Off-Grid or Lockdown without the unlock challenge, ways another app could drive PLANR's exported components, and anything that leaks data off the device.
