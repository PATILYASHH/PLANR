# Changelog

All notable changes to PLANR. Full notes for each release are in [release-notes/](release-notes/) and on the [Releases](https://github.com/PATILYASHH/PLANR/releases) page.

## Official release: 5 December 2026
- PLANR launches on **Google Play** and **F-Droid**, signed with the permanent ExoSynk Labs key.

## 0.6.0 (unreleased)
- Share into PLANR (share sheet and text-selection menu) with smart parsing.
- Phone calendar events on Today (read-only, on-device).
- Light theme, Material You colours and an Appearance setting.
- Search across reminders, notes, alarms and routines.
- Quick Settings tiles (Focus, Quick add, Off-Grid) and Today / Quick add widgets.
- Daily auto-backup to a chosen folder (keeps 7), named like XPENC's for CONECTR.
- Hinglish smart input ("kal 9 baje parth ko call karna").
- First-run walkthrough; on-device crash reports with Share in About.

## 0.5.3 (folded into 0.6.0)
- Off-Grid: true full-screen lock; navigation and status bars can't be used to leave it.
- Off-Grid: incoming calls always come through, full screen with phone access allowed.
- Repeating reminders keep alerting when one is missed.
- Releases are signed with the permanent ExoSynk Labs key (see docs/SIGNING.md).

## 0.5.2 (2026-11-14)
- Official release date (5 December 2026) shown in **About PLANR**.
- GitHub releases are full releases now, so "latest" links always work.
- New README, Code of Conduct, Security and Privacy policies, issue forms and docs.

## 0.5.1 (2026-11-10)
- PLANR is **open source** under the Apache License 2.0 (LICENSE, NOTICE, CONTRIBUTING).
- **About PLANR**: version, source, license, privacy, credits, and *More from us* (FITNS, XPENC, CONECTR → exosynk.com/exosynk-labs).

## 0.5.0 (2026-11-10)
- **Full-screen alarms**: wake the screen and fill it; Alarm setup checklist; 10-second test alarm; new ring screen with swipe to dismiss/snooze.
- **Smart input**: "call to Parth at 9 am", "wake me at 6:30 weekdays", "note: …", with contact matching and Call/Message/WhatsApp buttons.
- **Today** tab: week/month calendar of reminders, notes and alarms, plus a day agenda and quick actions.

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-10)
- Five modules in one app: **Habit, Alarm, Reminders, Notes, Routines**.
- Alarm groups, snooze rules, vibration patterns, read-aloud, math dismiss, morning brief.
- Reminders with categories, repeats, early and alarm-style alerts; notes and checklists; if-then routines with undo.
- Includes the unreleased 0.3 work: Mindful Gateway, open limits, emergency passes, Lockdown, Wind Down, Eye Care, Insights, XP, widget, backup & restore, real database migrations.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-10)
- First public preview: Off-Grid Mode, scheduled Off-Grid (Bedtime), Focus (Pomodoro), Shields, Schedules, dashboard and uninstall protection.
