## PLANR 0.4.0 — an ExoSynk Labs project

PLANR is now a full planner: **Habit · Alarm · Reminders · Notes · Routines** in one offline app, and they work together.

### Habit
Everything from earlier versions (shields, Mindful Gateway, focus, schedules, Off-Grid, Lockdown, Insights, XP) now lives in the Habit module. Its Home now has a **Today** card with your next alarm, what's due, pinned notes and running routines.

### Alarm
- Alarm groups: switch a whole set of alarms on or off at once (e.g. "Weekday mornings").
- Repeat on chosen days, or ring once on a specific date. Skip just the next ring.
- Alarm sound from your ringtones (or silent), per-alarm volume, gradual volume increase.
- 5 vibration patterns, snooze interval (5–30 min) and repeat count (1–5 times or forever).
- Read the time and alarm name aloud.
- Optional math challenge to dismiss.
- Morning brief after you dismiss: today's reminders (tick them off right there), habit streak, next alarm, running routines.
- Rings over the lock screen and during Off-Grid. Stops after 5 minutes (snoozes if it can).

### Reminders
- With a date and time, all-day, or "anytime" (no date). Repeat daily, on weekdays, weekly, monthly or yearly.
- Early alert (5 min to 1 day before), Complete and Snooze right from the notification.
- Alarm-style alerts that ring full screen for the important ones.
- Categories with colours, pinned reminders, Today / Scheduled / Anytime / All / Completed views, quick add.

### Notes
- Text notes and checklists, 8 colours, pin, archive, search.
- **Remind me** on a note creates a linked reminder; reminders show and open their note.
- During Off-Grid, jot a thought from the lock screen. It's saved as a pinned note.

### Routines
- If → Then, with **undo when it ends** (like Samsung Modes & Routines).
- Triggers: time or time window, alarm dismissed, charging, battery low, headphones, app opened, Off-Grid start/end, focus finished, or manual.
- Actions: Do Not Disturb, sound mode, media volume, start Off-Grid, start Focus, Mindful Gateway, alarm group on/off, a message, today's brief, open an app.
- Suggestions to start from: Good morning, Bedtime, Desk focus, Headphones on, After Off-Grid.

### Also
- XP for completing reminders and for getting up without snoozing; new achievements "Getting it done" and "Early bird".
- The widget shows your next alarm and how many reminders are due.
- Backups now include alarms, reminders, notes and routines (0.3 backups still restore).
- Alarms, reminders and routine timers are re-registered after reboots, updates and time-zone changes.

### Also new since 0.2 (from the unreleased 0.3)
- **Mindful Gateway:** an optional pause before every app, with a skip list.
- **Shields:** daily open limits, plus 0–5 emergency passes a day.
- **Lockdown:** hours when rules can only get stricter, optionally blocking Android Settings.
- **Wind Down** bedtime reminder with auto Do Not Disturb, and **Eye Care 20-20-20**.
- **Insights:** 5-week heatmap, per-app history, XP, levels and achievements, plus a home-screen widget.
- **Backup & restore** to a JSON file.
- Upgrades keep your data (real database migrations instead of resets).

### Install
Download `PLANR-0.4.0.apk` below. Android 11 or newer. Still no internet permission, so nothing leaves your phone.
For reliable alarms, allow notifications and (on Android 14+) full-screen alarms. The Alarm tab shows a button for each.
