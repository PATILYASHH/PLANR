## PLANR 0.5.0 — an ExoSynk Labs project

Alarms that always fill the screen, a calendar for your whole day, and smart input that understands what you type.

### Alarms ring full screen
- The alarm now **switches the screen on and fills it**, over the lock screen, even when the phone was asleep.
- With **Display over other apps** allowed, it also takes over the screen while you're using another app.
- PLANR keeps the phone awake from the moment the alarm fires until it's on screen, so it can't be dropped half-way.
- New **Alarm setup** checklist in the Alarm tab, with a one-tap fix for each item: notifications, full-screen alarms, exact alarms, display over other apps, battery.
- **Test alarm** button: rings in 10 seconds so you can lock the phone and check.
- Redesigned ring screen: big clock and date, a pulsing alarm icon, Samsung-style **swipe to dismiss / swipe to snooze**, and true full screen (status and navigation bars hidden).

### Smart input
Type it the way you'd say it, on the new Today tab or in Reminders:
- “**call to parth at 9 am**” → reminder “Call Parth” at 9:00, with Parth matched from your **contacts**.
- “message Rahul about the project tomorrow 4pm”, “whatsapp mom tonight”
- “wake me up at 6:30 weekdays” → alarm · “in 20 min check the oven” → reminder
- “pay rent on the 1st”, “gift shopping 25th december 10am”, “vitamins every day at 9:30 pm”
- “note: gift ideas for mom” → note

You see what PLANR understood before you save, and you can switch between reminder, alarm and note. For “call / message / WhatsApp someone” there's a **contact selection bar**: matching contacts appear as chips, and a search box shows when the name isn't found.

Reminders with a contact get a **Call / Message / WhatsApp button** in the notification, on the full-screen alert and in the calendar. In the reminder editor, typing a time in the title offers to set it for you.

Contacts are read on the phone only, after you allow it. PLANR still has no internet permission.

### Today + calendar
The new **Today** tab is the first thing you see:
- **Week or month calendar** with dots for reminders, notes and alarms. Repeating reminders show on every day they recur.
- Tap a day to see its alarms, reminders (tick them off, or call straight from the list) and notes. Overdue items are listed too.
- The smart bar adds to whichever day you picked.
- **Quick actions:** Alarm, Reminder, Note, Focus, Off-Grid, Routines.
- Screen time, level and streak at a glance.

The bottom bar is now **Today · Habit · Alarm · Reminders · Notes**. Routines open from Today's quick actions.

### Install
Download `PLANR-0.5.0.apk` below (Android 11+). After installing, open **Alarm → Alarm setup** and tap **Allow** on each item, then try **Test alarm**.
