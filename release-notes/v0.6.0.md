## PLANR 0.6.0 — an ExoSynk Labs project

### 📅 Official release: 5 December 2026

### New
- **Share into PLANR.** Share text from WhatsApp, Messages or email, or select text anywhere and tap **PLANR**. "Dentist Tue 4pm" becomes a reminder in one tap.
- **Your phone's calendar on Today.** Google, Samsung and other calendar events appear next to your reminders, notes and alarms. Read-only, on this phone; turn it on from Today or Settings.
- **Light theme and Material You.** Pick System, Light or Dark in **Settings → Appearance**, and match your wallpaper's colours on Android 12+.
- **Search everything.** The 🔍 on Today finds reminders, notes, alarms and routines.
- **Quick Settings tiles:** Focus (start/stop 25 min), Quick add and Off-Grid.
- **Two new widgets:** Today (next alarm and what's due) and Quick add (Reminder, Alarm, Note, Focus).
- **Daily auto-backup** to a folder you choose (keeps the last 7), in **Settings → Backup & restore**.
- **Hinglish smart input.** "kal 9 baje parth ko call karna", "roz raat 10 baje dawai", "subah 6 baje alarm laga do", "har somvar meeting".
- **First-run walkthrough** for new installs: what's in PLANR, theme, and every permission with the reason.
- **Crash reports** stay on your phone; share them from **About** if PLANR ever stops.

### Off-Grid is now really locked (from 0.5.3)
- The lock screen is true full screen. Back, Home and Recents can't take you out, and quick settings can't be pulled down.
- Incoming calls always come through, full screen with phone access allowed.

### Fixes
- Repeating reminders keep alerting even when one wasn't ticked off.

### Signed with the ExoSynk Labs key
Builds up to 0.5.2 used temporary keys. Once only: **export a backup**, uninstall the old PLANR, install this one, and restore.
