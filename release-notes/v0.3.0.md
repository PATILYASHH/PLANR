## PLANR 0.3.0 — an ExoSynk Labs project

A big update: stronger shields, Lockdown, bedtime routines, stats, XP and backups.

### New
- **Mindful Gateway:** an optional short pause before *every* app you open, not just shielded ones. Pick apps that skip it.
- **Open limits:** cap how many times a day you can continue past a shield.
- **Emergency passes:** a few a day (you choose 0–5) to open an app that's over its limit or blocked by a schedule or focus block. They never work during Off-Grid.
- **Lockdown:** set hours when rules can only get stricter. No pausing shields, deleting schedules or stopping focus. Lockdown can also block Android Settings, so PLANR can't be switched off there.
- **Wind Down:** a bedtime reminder, with optional Do Not Disturb that turns itself off in the morning.
- **Eye Care 20-20-20:** a reminder after every 20 minutes of continuous screen time.
- **Insights:** a 5-week screen-time heatmap, top apps for the week, and a 14-day chart per app. Daily history is now kept beyond Android's one-week limit.
- **XP, levels and 12 achievements:** earn XP for Off-Grid and focus minutes, and for closing a shielded app. If you're upgrading, past Off-Grid sessions count.
- **Home-screen widget:** today's screen time (or when Off-Grid ends), plus your level, streak and focus time.
- **Backup & restore:** export shields, schedules, settings, XP and Off-Grid history to a JSON file and restore it on any phone. Nothing is uploaded.

### Fixes
- Upgrading no longer wipes your data. The database now migrates properly instead of being reset.
- Focus minutes could be counted twice when the app and the service updated the timer at the same moment.
- Screen time now includes an app that was already open at midnight.
- The shield editor and schedule editor now scroll on small screens.

### Install
1. Download `PLANR-0.3.0.apk` below and open it. Allow installs from your browser or file manager if Android asks.
2. Open PLANR → **Finish setup**, then turn on the Accessibility service and Usage access.
3. New in 0.3: open **Settings** (gear on Home) to turn on Gateway, Lockdown, Wind Down or Eye Care, and allow notifications for reminders.

Requires Android 11 or newer. Still no internet permission. Nothing leaves your phone.
