## PLANR 0.2.0 — an ExoSynk Labs project

First public preview of PLANR, an offline Android focus app.

### Features
- **Off-Grid Mode:** lock the whole phone for 15 min – 24 h. Calls and emergency calls always work, camera is optional, and you can keep up to 5 extra apps. Early unlock needs a typing, cooldown or math challenge.
- **Scheduled Off-Grid:** recurring windows, including a one-tap Bedtime template (11 PM – 7 AM).
- **Focus (Pomodoro):** focus/break cycles that block chosen apps during focus.
- **Shields:** a breathing pause, forced delay, daily limit and pass window per app.
- **Schedules:** block-list or allow-list by time and weekday.
- **Dashboard:** today's screen time, top apps, Off-Grid streak.
- **Uninstall protection:** optional, uses no device-admin powers.

### Install
1. Download `PLANR-0.2.0.apk` below and open it. Allow installs from your browser or file manager if Android asks.
2. Open PLANR → **Finish setup**, then turn on the Accessibility service and Usage access.

Requires Android 11 or newer. No internet permission — nothing leaves your phone.
